SGF to Music Video turns a Go/Baduk/Weiqi game record into a musical board animation. Moves create deterministic notes, so the same game and music settings produce the same melody. Your SGF, board rendering, audio, and video recording stay in your browser.

[Make a music video](https://sgf2mv.soumyak4.in/) · [Create SGF Postcards](https://sgf2mv.soumyak4.in/SGF-Postcards.html)

## Give your game a soundtrack

Load or paste an SGF, preview any move, and choose instruments, pace, mood, and board appearance. Simple mode offers a quick starting point; Complex mode adds control over musical mapping, board regions, move ranges, phrasing, and sustained layers.

Export the whole game or a selected clip as a **WebM video with audio**. Clip exports retain the preceding board position and musical context. Customize colors, stones, coordinates, move numbers, framing, and game details before recording. Chromium-based browsers are recommended for video export.

Optional on-device KataGo estimates add win-rate graphs, ownership shading, and predicted dead-stone marks. The engine and model load only when requested. These are neural estimates rather than a full tree search or final score.

## SGF Postcards

The [standalone postcard editor](https://sgf2mv.soumyak4.in/SGF-Postcards.html) makes a full-board image and a close-up from your game. Choose a position, style the board and typography, edit its caption, and export PNGs separately or together in a ZIP.

Postcards supports captures, setup stones, passes, rectangular boards, and variations. Results come from the SGF or your edited details; it does not estimate territory. Style settings can be remembered locally, while game files and player details are not saved by the postcard tool.
