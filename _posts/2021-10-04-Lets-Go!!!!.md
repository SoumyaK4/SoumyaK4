## For the Go Community

Let's Go!!!! my projects collection that brings together the resources and tools I use and build for studying, playing, and teaching Go/Baduk/Weiqi. Here are the main projects in one place:

### Weiqi Roadmap

A learning roadmap with beginner and SDK guides, a resource directory, and book recommendations organised by level and topic.

- [Explore the roadmap](https://weiqi.soumyak4.in/roadmap/)
- [Project updates](/project/Weiqi-Roadmap/)

### BadukTube

A searchable catalogue of 1,200+ free Go lectures, with rank, topic, and tag filters and curated study collections.

- [Browse BadukTube](https://baduktube.soumyak4.in/)
- [Project updates](/project/BadukTube/)

### OGS Script

Enhances the Online-Go.com board and review experience.

- [Get the Tampermonkey script](https://github.com/SoumyaK4/OGS-Tampermonkey)

### Sensei's Library Script

A Tampermonkey script that gives Sensei's Library a clean dark theme and opens SGFs in a compact BesoGo popup.

- [Get the Tampermonkey script](https://gist.github.com/SoumyaK4/527634f02694d3de9e73123f9c611726)

### SWHub

A Go study and play app developed as continuation from my WeiqiHub fork: Puzzles & SRS, KataGo, Game based reviews and puzzles, Pattern Search, Joseki/Fuseki, online play, and Tsumego Battles. Available for Windows, Linux, macOS, and Android. Some features require an engine, connection, or access password.

- [Download SWHub](https://github.com/SoumyaK4/SWHub/releases/latest)
- [Read the project overview](/project/SWHub/)

### Weiqi Tracker

Track your game data and make your progress visible over time.

- [Open Weiqi Tracker](https://wq-tracker.soumyak4.in/)

### SGF to Music Video

Turn an SGF game record into an elegant music video.

- [Create an SGF music video](https://sgf2mv.soumyak4.in/)


### Go Timer

An installable, offline-friendly Go clock that supports major time-control systems I made for my club meetups.

- [Open Go Timer](https://gotimer.soumyak4.in/)

### KaTrain Theme

A BadukTV-inspired KaTrain theme with JeffSounds.

- [Get the theme](https://github.com/SoumyaK4/Katrain-Theme-SoumyaK4)

### Free KataGo Guide

A practical guide to running free remote KataGo, with Colab and Modal notebooks for analysis and KataGo training contribution.

- [Current guides and notebooks](https://github.com/SoumyaK4/SWHub/tree/SWHub/Docs/Free%20Cloud%20Katago)
- [Original cloud-engine guide](https://gist.github.com/SoumyaK4/87336ad7553f4a80173ee477ebeba364)

### TsumeGod

A Telegram tsumego bot for quick, daily Go-puzzle practice.

- [Start practising](https://tsumegod.soumyak4.in/)
- [Project updates](/project/TsumeGod/)
- Inspired by [Skwidder's IGS Puzzle Bot](https://github.com/Skwidder/IGS-Puzzle-Bot).
