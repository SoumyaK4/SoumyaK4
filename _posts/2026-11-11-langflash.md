LangFlash is a free character flashcard app for memorizing Korean Hangul, Japanese Hiragana and Katakana, and simplified Chinese characters. Choose a reading from four options, check the feedback, and build a review habit with spaced repetition.

[Start practising](https://lang.soumyak4.in/) · [Browse character guides](https://lang.soumyak4.in/guides/)

## Choose your starting point

- **Hangul:** the 19 modern consonants and 21 vowels, with romanization notes.
- **Hiragana and Katakana:** basic kana, voiced forms, combinations, and additional reading marks and sounds.
- **Simplified Chinese:** standard characters with pinyin and source glosses where available, with an HSK recognition path starting at level 1.

Due reviews come before new characters. Missed characters return in an end review, and free practice lets you explore without changing your review schedule or statistics. The searchable library, daily goals, review forecast, and activity history help you choose what to study next.

## Keep your progress

No account is needed. Study history and settings stay in your browser, with backup export and restore in Settings. Install LangFlash for offline practice after its resources have been cached. Pronunciation uses installed local device voices when available.

This is character and reading practice; conversation, grammar, handwriting, and stroke order are outside its scope. Clearing browser data removes local progress, so export a backup before changing browsers or devices.
