Weiqi Roadmap started as my study notes and has grown into a guide for learning Go/Baduk/Weiqi. Follow the [rank-based roadmap](https://weiqi.soumyak4.in/roadmap/), browse the [resource directory](https://weiqi.soumyak4.in/posts/weiqi-resources/), or find [books for your level](https://weiqi.soumyak4.in/posts/books/).

The resource directory now has dedicated pages for places to play, databases, tools, puzzles, AI, video lectures, books, and paid or freemium resources.
