## [RhymeTime](https://rhymetime.soumyak4.in)

Made this on a whim, because I wanted to play a game where I could rhyme words but couldn't find anything good on the web

## Find your flow

RhymeTime has grown into a practice space for poets, rappers, and anyone who enjoys wordplay. Play without an account, bring your own prompt, or slow down and compare sounds in the rhyme lab.

| Mode | What you can do |
| --- | --- |
| [Word sprint](https://rhymetime.soumyak4.in/play/word) | Build a streak of rhymes in two minutes, with optional themes |
| [Verse session](https://rhymetime.soumyak4.in/play/sentence) | Write lines with end rhymes, internal rhymes, and multisyllabic matches |
| [Freestyle](https://rhymetime.soumyak4.in/play/free) | Start a challenge from your own word, phrase, or line |
| [Daily challenge](https://rhymetime.soumyak4.in/play/daily) | Try the shared daily prompt and goal |
| [Rhyme lab](https://rhymetime.soumyak4.in/lab) | Compare words and phrases without a timer and inspect their phonetic alignment |

The scoring compares pronunciations and syllables, including alternative readings and phrase endings. Unknown words use labeled estimates. Feedback helps with practice; your ear and delivery still matter.

Explore [rhyme techniques](https://rhymetime.soumyak4.in/learn), track personal statistics and achievements, or compare scores on the optional community leaderboard. Preferences and device scores stay in your browser; named rounds may be submitted to the global leaderboard when available. Clearing browser data removes local progress.
